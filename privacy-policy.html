Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Premium Pro Contractors is a trade name (DBA) of Premium Painting & Carpentry Corp, a Massachusetts corporation ("we", "us", "our"). We operate premiumprocontractors.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone number, project address).
  • Project details and messages you send us.
  • Usage data (pages visited, device and browser information).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your estimate requests and provide our contracting services.
  • To send transactional messages (appointment reminders, estimate confirmations, project updates).
  • With your separate consent, to send marketing and promotional messages.

SMS / Mobile Information

By providing your phone number and checking the applicable consent box on our website forms (Contact or Free Estimate pages), you opt in to receive SMS text messages from Premium Pro Contractors. We send two types of messages: (1) transactional messages such as appointment reminders, estimate and project confirmations, and account notifications; and (2) with your separate consent, marketing and promotional messages such as seasonal offers and discounts. The phone field is optional and SMS consent is never required to use our services. Message frequency may vary and message & data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This includes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. We do not share SMS opt-in information with subcontractors, service providers, affiliates, or third parties for their own marketing. Service providers may only help us operate our messaging program and customer communications under our instructions, and they are not permitted to use SMS opt-in data for any other purpose.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate the site, remember your preferences, analyze traffic, and improve your experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site features. We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our site. Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent are never collected for sale or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and apply reasonable safeguards (including HTTPS encryption) to protect it.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
