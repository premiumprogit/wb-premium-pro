Questions

General contractor in Peabody, MA: common questions

What does a general contractor do?

A general contractor plans, coordinates and delivers your whole project, from permits and materials to scheduling every trade and the final walkthrough. As a professional contractor, we stay a single point of accountability so you are not managing a painter, a plumber and a carpenter separately. It is one team, one schedule and one number.

How much does a general contractor cost in Peabody, MA?

Projects are priced by scope, not a flat hourly rate. As a guide, bathroom remodels run about $12,000 to $30,000, kitchens $25,000 to $70,000+, and additions start near $40,000. Your written, itemized estimate is free, so you see the cost before any work begins.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Premium Pro Contractors is a licensed and insured, family-run general contractor based at 9 Centennial Drive in Peabody, MA. Proof of license and insurance is provided before we start.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Peabody and the North Shore of Massachusetts, including Lynnfield, Marblehead, Beverly, Salem, Danvers and nearby towns. See all service areas.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Send your project details and we confirm scope, timing and next steps, then provide a free written, itemized quote.
