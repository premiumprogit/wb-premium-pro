Why North Shore homeowners choose Premium Pro Contractors

The North Shore of Massachusetts runs from the harbor towns of Salem, Beverly and Marblehead through the inland neighborhoods of Peabody, Lynnfield, Danvers and Wakefield, and every one of them has homes worth remodeling the right way. What ties them together is the same problem: too many projects stall because no single person is accountable. We fix that by keeping one contractor on your job from the first walkthrough to the last punch-list item, so nothing falls through the gap between trades.

We are a family-run, licensed and insured general contractor based in Peabody, and most of our North Shore work comes from referrals. That matters more than any ad. When a neighbor sees a kitchen finished right, a bathroom that was actually waterproofed or a paint job with clean lines that still look sharp two winters later, they call us for their own project. Referral-built means we cannot cut a corner and disappear, because the next job on the next street depends on this one being done right.

Kitchen and bathroom remodeling on the North Shore

Kitchens and bathrooms are where one contractor matters most, because cabinets, counters, plumbing, electrical, tile and finish carpentry all have to land on the same schedule. We plan a kitchen around how you actually cook and gather, then run every trade so the room feels finished, not assembled. Bathrooms punish shortcuts even harder: a remodel that looks fine on day one but was not waterproofed correctly turns into rot behind the tile a year later. We build them the slow-is-fast way, with proper substrate, a clean tile layout, quality valves and ventilation that actually moves moisture out. Explore kitchen remodeling and bathroom remodeling for full scope details.

Additions and whole-home remodeling

When a North Shore family needs more space, an addition usually beats moving, especially with what homes here are worth near Salem, Beverly and Marblehead. We match rooflines, siding and trim so new work reads as part of the original house instead of a bolt-on. On larger whole-home remodels, one accountable contractor keeps framing, drywall, electrical and finish carpentry moving in the right order, so the project has a single schedule and a single number to plan around. See home additions and framing.

Painting, cabinets and carpentry

Good painting is mostly good prep. The difference between a repaint that lasts and one that chips at the edges a few months later is in the patching, sanding and clean cut lines, not just the color. On North Shore homes near the coast, exterior painting means surfaces prepped to hold up against salt air and real New England weather. Cabinet refinishing is one of the highest-return updates you can make without a full kitchen rebuild, and our finish carpenters handle doors, crown, baseboard, wainscoting and built-ins that give older North Shore homes their character. Permits, inspections and Massachusetts code

A lot of remodeling stress comes from permits and inspections, not the building itself. As a licensed Massachusetts general contractor, we pull the right permits for your North Shore project, build to current code, and coordinate the inspection schedule so nothing stalls halfway through. That protects you at resale, too: work done without permits can become a problem the day you sell. Because one contractor owns the paperwork as well as the build, the only thing you have to think about is the finished room.

Planning and budgeting your North Shore remodel

The best remodels start before the first swing of a hammer. When we walk your North Shore home, we talk through what you want to change, what has to stay, and what the realistic budget is, so the proposal reflects the actual project instead of a guess. We are honest when a smaller scope will get you most of the result: sometimes a repaint and a cabinet refresh transform a kitchen for far less than a gut renovation, and sometimes it is worth doing the addition once, correctly. Because one contractor owns the whole job, we can phase the work if that fits your budget better, and there is a single number to plan around, not a stack of separate bills from trades who never talk to each other. That clarity, up front and in writing, is why so much of our North Shore work turns into referrals to the next town over.
