Kitchen remodeling in Danvers split-levels and ranches

Split-levels and ranches built in the postwar decades are all over Putnamville and the Danvers Highlands. Their kitchens are often closed off and short on counter space. Moving a doorway, opening a wall to the dining room or turning a peninsula into an island usually changes more than any finish upgrade. See our kitchen remodeling in Danvers page for the full scope.

Opening a load-bearing wall in a Danvers home

Many Danvers homeowners want the kitchen and living room connected. If the wall carries load, we plan a properly sized beam, support the ceiling during the work, reroute wiring and ductwork, and have the framing inspected before the walls close.

Bathroom remodeling in older Danvers Colonials

Colonials near Danvers Square and Tapleyville often have one full bath upstairs that has been updated in layers. We strip it to the studs, fix the subfloor, waterproof the shower and build it back with tile, a new vanity and better ventilation.

Adding a bedroom: dormers and additions in Danvers

When a Cape or ranch runs out of room, a dormer or a rear addition often costs less than moving. We handle the framing, roofing tie-ins, windows, siding and interior finish so the new space looks like it was always there.

Finished basements and lower levels

Split-level lower floors and Colonial basements can become a family room, office or guest space. We look at moisture first, then frame, insulate, run licensed electrical, hang drywall and finish the space.

Interior painting for Danvers homes

Interior painting starts with patching, sanding and priming, then clean cut lines and durable finishes on walls, ceilings and trim. Our house painters in Danvers page covers the details.

Exterior painting and trim near Danversport

Homes near Danversport and the rivers see more moisture than inland streets, which shows up first in window sills, corner boards and porch floors. We replace rotted wood before painting so the new finish is not covering a problem.

Cabinet painting versus new cabinets in Danvers

If your cabinet boxes are solid and the layout works, painting them with a sprayed finish updates the kitchen without a tear-out. If the boxes are failing or the layout does not work, replacement is the honest answer, and we will tell you which one applies.

Decks, porches and stairs

Winter freeze and thaw loosens railings, heaves footings and rots stair stringers. We repair or rebuild decks, porches and exterior stairs so they are safe and code compliant.

Ice dams, ceiling repairs and insulation

After a hard Danvers winter we often see stained ceilings and damaged drywall from ice dams. We repair the drywall, check the insulation and ventilation in that area and repaint so the ceiling matches.

Preparing a Danvers home for sale

Before listing, a fresh coat of paint, repaired trim and a few carpentry fixes can make a big difference in how a house shows. Realtors like Matt Munson, whose video is on this page, call us for exactly this kind of work.
