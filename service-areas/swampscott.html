Remodeling around Swampscott's coastal climate

The ocean that makes Swampscott so desirable is also the single biggest factor in how a home ages here. Salt-laden air corrodes fasteners, degrades paint, and works its way into any gap in the building envelope, while the freeze-thaw swing of a New England winter opens seams that a milder climate would forgive. That is why a remodel in Swampscott has to be planned differently than the same project two towns inland. On exterior work we prioritize corrosion-resistant hardware, proper flashing details and coatings rated for coastal exposure, and we do the unglamorous prep that determines whether a finish lasts three years or thirteen. On decks and railings we detail every connection to shed water instead of holding it, because a deck that traps moisture near the ocean is a deck that rots from the fasteners out. Inside, older Swampscott homes often carry the effects of decades near the water, from tired window casings to plaster that has moved, and we address the cause, not just the cosmetic symptom, so the repair actually holds. Choosing materials and methods for the climate is not an upsell here, it is the difference between a Swampscott project that stays right and one you are calling someone back to fix.
