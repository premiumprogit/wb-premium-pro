Bathroom remodeling in Reading

Bathrooms are small rooms that punish shortcuts. A remodel that looks fine on day one but was not waterproofed correctly turns into rot behind the tile a year later, and in an older Reading home that hidden damage can spread into framing you never see. We build bathrooms the slow-is-fast way: correct substrate and waterproofing, a clean tile layout, quality valves and fixtures, and ventilation that actually moves moisture out. Whether it is a tired hall bath, a dated primary suite or a cramped half bath you want reworked, one crew handles demo, plumbing, tile and finish so the schedule holds and the details line up. See bathroom remodeling for a full scope.

A bathroom built to last: proper waterproofing under the tile, quality fixtures and clean finish work.

Interior and exterior painting in Reading

Good painting is mostly good prep, and that is doubly true in older Reading homes where plaster, settled trim and past repairs all have to be dealt with before a brush touches the wall. Interior painting means patching, sanding, priming repairs and cutting clean lines so the finish stays sharp in a lived-in home instead of chipping at the edges a few months later. Exterior painting on a New England home means surfaces scraped, prepped and coated to hold up to real freeze-thaw winters and summer sun, not just a fresh coat over failing paint. Because we are a full general contractor, the carpentry or drywall a paint job uncovers gets handled by the same team.

Interior painting done as finish work: proper prep, primed repairs and clean, even coats that last.
