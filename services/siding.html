Siding and Exterior Repair

The Best Time to Fix Siding Is Before Water Gets Behind It.

Siding problems usually start quietly. A loose board, soft trim around a window, failing caulk or paint that keeps peeling in the same place can point to a bigger exterior envelope issue. Premium Pro Contractors helps North Shore homeowners inspect the outside of the home, repair what is failing and plan the right finish work after the structure is protected.

We are based in Peabody and work across Lynn, Danvers, Beverly, Salem and nearby towns. That local footprint matters because coastal weather, freeze-thaw cycles and older framing details all affect how siding and trim should be repaired.

Siding Work We Handle

  • Siding repair and selective replacement
  • Exterior trim replacement and rot repair
  • Window and door casing repairs
  • Caulking, sealing and moisture-prone detail work
  • Fascia, soffit and exterior carpentry coordination
  • Painting prep after siding or trim work is complete

Experience note: because Premium Pro Contractors also handles carpentry, painting, decks and remodeling, siding repairs can be scoped with the rest of the home instead of treated as an isolated patch.

When Siding Should Be Part of a Larger Plan

If the trim is failing, we may connect the scope to carpentry and trim repair. If the exterior finish is weathered, the repair may lead naturally into house painting or exterior painting. If the back of the house is being opened for a larger project, the siding scope can be coordinated with home additions or deck and patio work.

We serve homeowners across Lynn, Danvers, Peabody and the broader North Shore service area.

Exterior Envelope

What We Check Before Recommending a Fix

01

Moisture Paths

We look at where water is entering or sitting, especially around windows, doors, trim joints and roofline transitions.

02

Soft Trim

Replacing siding while leaving rotted trim behind creates another repair later. The surrounding detail gets checked first.

03

Paint Failure

Repeated peeling can be a siding, moisture or prep issue. We identify the cause before recommending another coat.

04

Scope Fit

Some homes need a focused repair. Others need a coordinated exterior plan. The estimate should make that distinction clear.
