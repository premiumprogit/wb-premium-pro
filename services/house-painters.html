Painting Contractor

Clean Finish Is the Result. Prep Is the Reason.

Most homeowners do not call a painter because they want a color change. They call because the current finish is peeling, the trim looks tired, the old caulk failed, or the house needs to feel cared for again before more damage shows up. Premium Pro Contractors approaches painting like a general contractor, not a quick coating crew.

That matters on North Shore homes. Older homes in Lynn and Salem often need trim repair before paint. Peabody and Danvers homes may have sun-baked siding, moisture around window casings or previous paint that was never scraped correctly. Our house painters look at those details before we quote, because paint only lasts when the surface underneath is ready.

What We Paint

  • Interior walls, ceilings, trim and doors
  • Exterior siding, shutters, fascia and entry doors
  • Cabinet painting and kitchen refreshes
  • Deck railings, porch details and exterior trim
  • Drywall patching, sanding and small carpentry prep
  • Color changes, rental turnovers and pre-sale refreshes

Experience note: Premium Pro Contractors is a family-run, licensed and insured general contractor based in Peabody. Painting is handled inside the same project standards as our remodeling, carpentry and exterior work.

Painting Services Linked to the Rest of the Home

House painting often uncovers related work. If the trim is soft, we can connect the scope to carpentry and trim repair. If exterior boards are failing, we can plan the next step with siding and exterior repair. If the project starts inside a kitchen or bathroom, we can coordinate painting with kitchen remodeling or bathroom remodeling instead of forcing you to manage multiple crews.

We serve homeowners across Lynn, Danvers, Peabody and the broader North Shore service area.
