Exterior Painting Built for the Beverly Coast

Why Prep Decides Whether a Beverly Paint Job Lasts

Almost every failed paint job, inside or out, traces back to prep, not paint. Indoors, patches that were not primed flash through the topcoat, and stains bleed back within weeks. Outdoors on the Beverly coast, paint applied over chalking, dirty or peeling surfaces simply will not bond, and salt-laden moisture works its way behind it and lifts it off. That is the number one reason a homeowner who tried a cheap crew or a DIY weekend ends up calling a general contractor to redo it properly.

Our prep is deliberately thorough, because thorough is what makes the finish last. Walls and trim are patched, sanded and spot-primed. Exteriors are washed, scraped, sanded, repaired, re-caulked and primed down to a sound base before a single finish coat goes on. It is not glamorous work, and it is the part a homeowner never sees, which is exactly why it is the part that gets skipped by cut-rate crews and never skipped by us.

Managing Moisture and Salt Air on Beverly Exteriors

Living close to the water in Beverly is beautiful, and it is hard on exterior paint.
