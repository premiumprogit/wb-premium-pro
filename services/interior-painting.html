Interior painting done right

Most paint jobs look fine from across the room. We make sure ours look right when you are standing two feet away. Interior painting starts with prep that nobody sees and everybody feels: cracks filled, nail holes patched, glossy surfaces scuffed, and everything taped and masked. We cover and protect your furniture and floors, prime where it is needed, cut clean lines at trim and ceilings, and lay down at least two coats of premium paint so the color is even and durable. Then we clean up at the end of every day so your home stays livable while we work. When only the kitchen needs a refresh, cabinet painting can transform the space for a fraction of a full remodel.

Exterior painting that holds up to New England weather

Exterior painting on North Shore homes is a different job than interior work, and cutting corners on it shows fast. Salt air, freeze-thaw and full summer sun punish a cheap coat within a season. We wash and prep the surface, scrape and sand failing paint, spot-prime bare wood, caulk the gaps that let water in, and use exterior-grade product rated for our climate. Siding, trim, porches, fascia and doors all get the same care, so the house looks sharp from the curb and stays that way for years. See exterior painting for how we handle a full exterior repaint.

Cabinet painting and kitchen refreshes

Cabinet painting is one of the highest-return updates you can make to a kitchen without a full rebuild. Done properly it is not a quick roller job: we remove doors and hardware, clean and degrease, sand and prime the faces, and spray or fine-brush a durable finish so the result reads factory-smooth, not brushed-on. For a lot of North Shore kitchens, refinished cabinets plus a fresh wall color deliver most of the visual impact of a remodel at a fraction of the cost. Explore cabinet painting for what a full cabinet project includes.

Wall repair and drywall patching before we paint

Paint does not hide a damaged wall, it highlights it. Before the first coat, we handle the repairs that make the finish read smooth: filling nail holes and anchor holes, patching cracks that open along seams, floating over dents and dings, and cutting out and re-taping drywall where it has failed. On older North Shore homes we often find settling cracks and popped nails that keep coming back if you just paint over them, so we address the cause, not only the surface. If a room needs more than cosmetic patching, we can handle the drywall and plaster work as part of the same job, which keeps you with one accountable contractor instead of chasing a separate handyman before the painter can start.

Ceilings, popcorn removal and trim work

Ceilings get ignored until they are the only thing that looks off in a freshly painted room. We repaint ceilings flat white or your chosen shade, cut clean where they meet the walls, and can scrape and refinish dated popcorn ceilings for a smoother, more current look. Trim, doors, baseboard, crown and window casings are their own kind of finish work: they take a different sheen, a steadier hand and more patience at the lines than open wall area does. Because we are a general contractor as well as a painting company, trim carpentry and paint come from the same crew, so a chipped or damaged piece of trim gets repaired, not just painted around.

Wallpaper removal and surface prep

Painting over wallpaper is a shortcut that shows within weeks as bubbling seams and lifting edges. When a room has wallpaper you want gone, we remove it properly, clean the adhesive residue off the wall, skim and sand the surface flat, and prime before any color goes on. It is more work up front, and it is the difference between walls that look freshly built and walls that look papered-then-painted. The same goes for glossy or previously oil-based surfaces, which need to be de-glossed and primed so the new paint actually bonds instead of peeling later.

Color consultation and choosing the right sheen

Picking a color off a two-inch chip is how people end up repainting a room twice. We help you test real samples on your actual walls, in your actual light, because North Shore homes see very different light in a south-facing living room than in a north-facing bedroom. Just as important as the color is the sheen: flat and matte hide wall imperfections but are harder to wipe, eggshell and satin balance durability and looks for most living spaces, and semi-gloss stands up to scrubbing on trim, doors, kitchens and baths. We match sheen to how each room actually gets used, so the finish holds up without looking plasticky.

Low-VOC and low-odor paint options

If you have kids, pets, allergies or someone working from home, paint fumes matter. We offer low-VOC and near-zero-VOC interior products from Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams that cut the odor and let you use a room again far sooner, without giving up durability or coverage. For families that want to stay in the house during an interior project, this is often the difference between a livable week and a miserable one, and we plan the sequence of rooms so you always have a clean, aired-out space to retreat to.

Exterior prep: power washing, scraping and caulking

Most exterior paint failures are prep failures. Before a brush touches your siding we power wash off dirt, chalk and mildew, scrape and sand any peeling or failing paint back to a sound edge, spot-prime bare wood so it does not wick moisture, and caulk the joints and gaps where water sneaks behind the coating. On North Shore homes that face salt air and hard winters, this prep is what buys you years of protection instead of a single good-looking season. When the exterior needs a full wash before painting or on its own, our power washing crew handles it as part of the plan.

Because we also handle home additions and remodeling, the painting is coordinated with the build schedule instead of tacked on at the end by an outside crew who has to work around everyone else.

How long does fresh paint last?

Done right, interior paint on walls typically holds up 5 to 10 years, trim and doors a bit longer, and high-traffic spots like hallways, kids rooms and kitchens somewhat less because they take more abuse. Exterior paint on North Shore homes usually lasts 7 to 12 years depending on exposure, surface and product, with sun-facing and weather-facing sides aging fastest. The two biggest factors are prep and product, which is exactly where a cheap job cuts corners. When we quote a project we tell you honestly what to expect from the finish, so the number in the estimate reflects real longevity, not a coat that looks great on day one and tired by next winter.

Lead-safe painting on older North Shore homes

Plenty of homes on the North Shore were built before 1978, which means the old paint layers can contain lead. Disturbing that paint with careless scraping or sanding is a real hazard for kids and pets. We follow lead-safe work practices on pre-1978 homes: contained work areas, proper surface prep, HEPA cleanup and correct disposal, so the job protects your family instead of spreading dust through the house. If your home is older, tell us up front and we build the right precautions into the plan and the estimate.

Staining and finishing decks, railings and fences

Not everything gets paint. Decks, railings, pergolas and fences usually call for stain or a clear or semi-transparent finish that protects the wood while letting the grain show. We clean and prep the surface, address any failing finish, and apply a product rated for New England weather and foot traffic. Because we also build and repair decks and patios, we can handle any board or railing repairs before the finish goes on, so you are not sealing over rot.

Interior painting works year-round, since we control the environment inside. If you are planning an exterior repaint, reaching out early lets us book the right weather window instead of squeezing the job into a bad one.
