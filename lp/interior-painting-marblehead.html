FAQ

Interior Painting in Marblehead, MA

How much does interior painting cost in Marblehead?

Most interior painting runs about $2 to $6 per square foot of floor area, so a single bedroom often lands around $450 to $900 and a whole-home repaint runs into the thousands. It depends on ceiling height, wall repair, whether trim and ceilings are included, and the finish you choose. Your written, itemized estimate is free, so you see the price by room before any work begins.

Can we stay in the house while you paint?

Almost always, yes. We work room by room, seal off the active area with dust containment, protect your floors and furniture, and clean up every day so the rest of the house stays livable.

Do you use low-VOC or low-odor paint?

Yes. For interior work in Marblehead homes we default to low-VOC, low-odor Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams paints, which matters when families are living in the house. Most rooms are comfortable to use again the same day.

Do you paint ceilings and trim, or only walls?

We paint walls, ceilings, trim, doors, closets and stairwells, and you can pick the scope room by room. We price each element separately so you decide what is worth including.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Premium Pro Contractors is a family-run, licensed and insured general contractor based in Peabody at 9 Centennial Drive. Because we are a full general contractor, any wall repair, drywall, plaster or carpentry that comes up is handled by the same accountable crew.
