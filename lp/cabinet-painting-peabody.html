FAQ

Cabinet Painting in Peabody, MA

How much does cabinet painting cost in Peabody?

Most full kitchen cabinet painting jobs on the North Shore run $3,500 to $8,000, depending on the number of doors and drawer fronts, the condition of the boxes and the finish you choose. A single island or a smaller kitchen can fall under that. Your written, itemized estimate is free so you see the number before any work begins.

Should I paint or replace my cabinets?

If the boxes are solid and the layout works, painting gets you a like-new kitchen for a fraction of replacement, which often starts at $15,000 or more. If the boxes are failing or the layout is wrong, replacement inside a full remodel makes more sense. As a general contractor we do both, so we give you an honest recommendation instead of selling you the bigger job.

How durable is a painted cabinet finish?

Durability comes from prep and product, not just the color. We degrease and sand every surface, prime with a bonding primer and spray a hard cabinet-grade finish, which holds up to daily kitchen use far better than a brushed-on wall paint. Done this way it stays smooth and washable for many years.

How long does it take?

A typical North Shore cabinet project takes about 4 to 7 working days from removal to reinstall. We confirm the exact schedule in your written proposal and keep the kitchen usable as much as possible while the doors are out.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Premium Pro Contractors is a family-run, licensed and insured general contractor based in Peabody. Because we are a full general contractor, we can paint your cabinets on their own or fold them into a complete kitchen remodel, with one accountable crew.
